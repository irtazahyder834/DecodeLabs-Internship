Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains how Savoria uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and ordering platform.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember information about your visit.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — required for core functionality such as keeping items in your cart and keeping you logged in.
  • Preference cookies — remember settings such as your preferred delivery address or menu filters.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand which pages and dishes are most popular so we can improve the site.

3. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect your ability to place orders or use saved reservations.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Our payment and delivery-tracking partners may set their own cookies when you interact with embedded checkout or map features. These are governed by the respective third party's own policy.

5. Contact

Questions about our use of cookies can be sent to hello@savoria.example.
