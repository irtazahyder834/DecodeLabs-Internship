Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Savoria ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and ordering platform, why we collect it, and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide when making a reservation or placing an order (name, phone, email, delivery address).
  • Order history, including items ordered, order value, and preferences.
  • Technical data such as browser type and pages visited, collected via cookies (see our Cookie Policy).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process reservations, orders, and deliveries.
  • To send confirmation emails and order status updates.
  • To improve our menu, service, and website experience.
  • To send optional marketing communications, which you may opt out of at any time.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Limited information (such as delivery address) is shared with our delivery riders solely to fulfil your order. Payment details are processed by third-party payment providers and are never stored on our servers.

4. Data Retention

Account and order data is retained for as long as your account is active, or as required to comply with legal and tax obligations.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@savoria.example.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to hello@savoria.example or 12-C Zamzama Boulevard, Clifton, Karachi.
